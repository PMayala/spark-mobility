// Driver FAQ

Common questions

Everything else, our team can answer on +250 796 698 668.

How and when do I get paid?

Instantly per ride inside the driver app, with weekly settlement to your account. You always see today's earnings in real time.

Do I choose my own hours?

Yes — there are no fixed shifts. Go online whenever you want. Lease payments run six days a week; the seventh day is yours either way.

What happens if the vehicle breaks down?

Maintenance is included on lease plans and handled by Spark's technicians, so repairs don't come out of your pocket.

When does the vehicle become mine?

At the end of the lease term — roughly two years for a moto, five for a car — ownership is transferred to you. No hidden balloon payment.

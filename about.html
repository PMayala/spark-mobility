// About Spark

We're building the electric backbone of African cities — starting with ours.

Spark Systems Rwanda Ltd runs Kigali's 100% electric ride network: the platform that moves riders, the lease program that puts vehicles in drivers' hands, and the operation that keeps the whole fleet running.

// What we operate

Three businesses, one network

Spark isn't just an app — we own, lease and maintain the vehicles behind it.

Ride-hailing platform

A digital platform connecting passengers with verified drivers — upfront fares, GPS tracking, digital payments.

Operating lease program

We own a fleet of electric mototaxis and EVs and lease them to qualified drivers on drive-to-own terms.

Fleet management

Charging, maintenance and telemetry for every leased vehicle — handled by our own technicians.

How a driver gets vetted

No one drives for Spark without clearing every step — owner-partners and lease drivers alike.

Application & ID

Identity confirmed against national ID.

Police check

Background clearance and a clean record, verified.

Licence verified

Category A or B licence checked and recorded.

Training

EV operation, road safety and customer service.

Gear & go

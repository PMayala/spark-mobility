// The policy moment

Rwanda chose electric. Spark was built for it.

2019

Electric taxis reach Kigali

The first electric taxi pilots put e-mobility on Rwanda's roads and on the national agenda.

Formalisation

Moto taxis go professional

Regulators require motorbike taxis to operate under formal companies or cooperatives — raising standards across the sector.

Jan 2025

Electric-only licensing

From January 2025, only electric motorcycles may be licensed for passenger service in Kigali, and new petrol taxi registrations end.

Today

Spark runs 100% electric

A full network — motos, sedans and crossovers — operating across all three districts, fully compliant with RURA and RTDA requirements.

Next

Beyond Kigali

Expansion to more Rwandan cities is planned as the network and charging footprint grow.
